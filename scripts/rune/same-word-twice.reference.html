Identical letters. Identical sung note. The rune differs because the bass moved — C sits a ♭7 above D and a 4 above G, so the same syllable is restless the first time and resolving the second. A lyric video reading only the audio cannot know this. Reading the key alone cannot either: against C minor both are the tonic. It is only visible against the bass, which is why the interval shown is the one to the bass and not to the key.
