Humid, sultry alt-R&B ballad at a half-time ~99 BPM, set on the Hàn River in Da Nang. Bilingual English/Vietnamese duet: Kizuna Sato leads — breathy close-mic whispers opening into warm, unforced belts — answered by a male melodic-rap verse, the two trading lines mid-sentence between languages. Deep 808 sub as a slow heartbeat, clean reverbed guitar harmonics, night-city ambience (distant motorcycles, river water). Builds to a dragon-fire peak with widened guitars and accelerating percussion, then drops to near-silence for a water-spray bridge with only falling water and clean guitar before the full band returns warm rather than loud. Chapter IV of The Fire Cycle — the answer record to "I Won't Be Your Fire": the fire goes out and she stays anyway. Gold and ember against blue-black water. Tender, not tragic.
